Are you you? Type it and see.

People type their own name, birthday and ZIP code from motor memory. Someone using a stolen identity has to read, recall or compute each answer, and that leaves gaps in the keystroke rhythm. Fill in both forms below and compare your two rhythms.

The step-up budget

A score does not convict anyone. A platform sends the riskiest share of new applicants to a slower check (document scan, selfie, call-back) and lets everyone else through. These curves come from re-running Monaro et al.'s published classifier on their 151 online participants. Pick a fraud rate and a budget and read off what the screen buys.

(2018), "Covert lie detection using keyboard dynamics," Scientific Reports 8:1976, from the authors' posted data.
