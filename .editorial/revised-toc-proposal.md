## Organizing principle (stated explicitly for the first time)
Marketing scholarship has three domains (Scheer; MacInnis 2011; Hunt; Yadav 2010):
**Constructs** (the concepts we theorize) · **Substantive domains** (the phenomena we study) ·
**Methodology** (how we measure, identify, infer). The book's parts already mirror this — we
will *name* it (Intro + a short preface per part) and *balance* it.

## Narrative arc (reader-optimal order)
Foundations → Constructs (what we measure) → Substantive Domains (what we study) →
Methodology (how we study it) → Unstructured & Multimodal Data → Integrative Seminars →
Research Craft → Cases.
