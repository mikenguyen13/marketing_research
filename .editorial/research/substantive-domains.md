# Substantive Domains Inventory — Scoping the "Substantive Domains" Part

**Purpose:** A comprehensive, organized inventory of the substantive domains (real-world phenomena, contexts, and decision areas) a doctoral-level marketing reference book should cover, mapped against the book's current substantive chapters (08–22), with a prioritized gap list.

**Date:** 2026-06-23
**Author:** Editorial research (Claude)

---

## How to read this document

- **Scope note:** one line stating the phenomenon/decision area.
- **Anchor reference(s):** seminal or representative work(s) with **DOIs verified via the Crossref REST API (api.crossref.org)**. Only DOIs that resolved with matching metadata are listed. Where a canonical work has no Crossref DOI (e.g., HBR / MIT SMR practitioner pieces), this is stated explicitly and **no DOI is invented**.
- **Coverage:** how the book currently treats the domain — `Covered`, `Partial`, or `Gap` — with the chapter(s).

The book's current "Substantive Domains" part: `08-branding`, `09-online-environments`, `10-advertising`, `11-communication` (information theory, not WOM), `12-sales`, `13-clv`, `14-celebrity-endorsement`, `15-influencer-marketing`, `16-nudges`, `17-pricing`, `18-service`, `19-health`, `20-gaming`, `21-marketing-finance`, `22-privacy`. Some domains below are also touched in other parts (e.g., `04-innovation`, `05-market-entry`, `06-virality`, `35-industrial-organization`, `48-ai-ml-marketing`, `51-platforms`); these are noted where relevant.

> **Methodological note on Chapter 11.** The book's `11-communication` is the *mathematical* theory of communication (Shannon entropy, mutual information, KL divergence) — an information-theory methods chapter. It does **not** cover marketing communication phenomena (WOM, virality, content, IMC). That substantive territory is split across `06-virality`, `14`, `15`, and gaps below. Flagged in the gap list.

## Group A — Marketing-mix / managerial decisions

### A1. Product & new-product development (NPD)
Scope: idea generation, design, development process, launch, and product-line decisions.
- Cooper, "Stage-Gate Systems: A New Tool for Managing New Products," *Business Horizons*, 1990. DOI: `10.1016/0007-6813(90)90040-I`
- (Diffusion of the resulting innovations: see A-adjacent / Group E.) Bass, "A New Product Growth for Model Consumer Durables," *Management Science*, 1969. DOI: `10.1287/mnsc.15.5.215`
- **Coverage: Partial** — `04-innovation` covers diffusion/innovation; the NPD *process* (stage-gate, design, launch decisions) is a **gap** in the substantive part.

### A2. Branding & brand equity
Scope: brand meaning, customer-based brand equity, personality, architecture, extensions, valuation.
- Keller, "Conceptualizing, Measuring, and Managing Customer-Based Brand Equity," *Journal of Marketing*, 1993. DOI: `10.1177/002224299305700101`
- Aaker, "Dimensions of Brand Personality," *Journal of Marketing Research*, 1997. DOI: `10.1177/002224379703400304`
- Aaker & Keller, "Consumer Evaluations of Brand Extensions," *Journal of Marketing*, 1990. DOI: `10.1177/002224299005400102`
- **Coverage: Covered** — `08-branding` (equity, personality implied, architecture, extensions, valuation).

### A3. Pricing
Scope: WTP/reservation prices, price discrimination, promotions, reference prices, demand estimation/identification.
- Zeithaml, "Consumer Perceptions of Price, Quality, and Value," *Journal of Marketing*, 1988. DOI: `10.1177/002224298805200302`
- Dodds, Monroe & Grewal, "Effects of Price, Brand, and Store Information on Buyers' Product Evaluations," *Journal of Marketing Research*, 1991. DOI: `10.2307/3172866`
- **Coverage: Covered** — `17-pricing`.

### A4. Advertising & advertising response
Scope: ad effectiveness, persuasion routes, ad-stock/carryover, econometric measurement of ad effects.
- Tellis, "Advertising Exposure, Loyalty, and Brand Purchase: A Two-Stage Model of Choice," *Journal of Marketing Research*, 1988. DOI: `10.1177/002224378802500202`
- **Coverage: Covered** — `10-advertising` (behavioral, theoretical, econometric approaches).

### A5. Sales promotion & discounting
Scope: how promotions work, deal effects, promotion profitability, price-ending effects.
- Blattberg, Briesch & Fox, "How Promotions Work," *Marketing Science*, 1995. DOI: `10.1287/mksc.14.3.g122`
- Anderson & Simester, "Effects of \$9 Price Endings on Retail Sales: Evidence from Field Experiments," *Quantitative Marketing and Economics*, 2003. DOI: `10.1023/A:1023581927405`
- **Coverage: Partial** — promotions/discounting handled inside `17-pricing`; no dedicated promotion treatment.

### A6. Personal selling & salesforce management
Scope: salesperson performance determinants, compensation, territory/effort allocation, sales-force sizing.
- Churchill, Ford, Hartley & Walker, "The Determinants of Salesperson Performance: A Meta-Analysis," *Journal of Marketing Research*, 1985. DOI: `10.1177/002224378502200201` (alt JSTOR: `10.2307/3151357`)
- **Coverage: Partial** — `12-sales` exists; confirm whether it covers salesforce *management* (performance, comp, allocation) vs. sales *response/analytics*. Likely partial.

### A7. Distribution channels & channel relationships
Scope: channel structure, power/commitment, governance, vertical relationships.
- Anderson & Weitz, "The Use of Pledges to Build and Sustain Commitment in Distribution Channels," *Journal of Marketing Research*, 1992. DOI: `10.1177/002224379202900103`
- **Coverage: Gap** — no channels/distribution chapter. (Some channel theory appears in analytical-modeling chapters but not as a substantive domain.)

### A8. Retailing & omnichannel
Scope: store/assortment/format decisions, the future of retailing, multi- to omni-channel integration.
- Grewal, Roggeveen & Nordfält, "The Future of Retailing," *Journal of Retailing*, 2017. DOI: `10.1016/j.jretai.2016.12.008`
- Verhoef, Kannan & Inman, "From Multi-Channel Retailing to Omni-Channel Retailing," *Journal of Retailing*, 2015. DOI: `10.1016/j.jretai.2015.02.005`
- **Coverage: Gap** — retailing/omnichannel not a dedicated domain (online retail partly in `09`).

### A9. Packaging
Scope: package design as a communication and point-of-sale variable.
- Underwood, Klein & Burke, "Packaging communication: attentional effects of product imagery," *Journal of Product & Brand Management*, 2001. DOI: `10.1108/10610420110410531`
- **Coverage: Gap** — niche; likely fine to fold into branding/advertising rather than a standalone chapter.

### B7. Customer engagement

### B8. Customer churn/retention targeting

- **Coverage: Partial** — retention modeling in `13-clv`; targeting-effectiveness angle is a gap.

### Structural recommendation
- **Reframe or supplement Chapter 11.** `11-communication` is information theory, not marketing communication. The substantive "marketing communication" territory (IMC, WOM, content) is under-served. Either rename `11` to signal it is a methods chapter, or add a dedicated WOM/communication-phenomena chapter (Priority 1, #3) so the table of contents does not imply `11` covers communication strategy.
- **Promote AI from method to phenomenon.** Consider a short substantive AI chapter (or a section in `09`/`18`) distinct from the `48` methods treatment (Priority 1, #6).
